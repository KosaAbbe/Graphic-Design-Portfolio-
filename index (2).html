I judge design against the brief.

Every project starts with one question: what is this design supposed to do? Then I check it on four things, in this order.

Type
Can it be read quickly, and do the sizes and pairings show what matters?
Hierarchy
Does the eye land on the right thing first?
Colour
Does the palette hold together and stay legible for everyone?
Layout
Are grid, spacing and alignment doing their job?

I also write and refine AI image prompts, and review each result against the client's brief before it goes anywhere.
